Freedom of Personal Information

Everyone deserves the right to control how their personal data is collected, used, and shared by companies, programs, and organizations without invasive surveillance or exploitation.

← Back to Home

Why Freedom of Personal Information Matters

The freedom of personal information is fundamental to digital privacy rights and personal autonomy. When mega-corporations exploit their dominant market position through invasive data collection, targeted surveillance, manipulative algorithms, and opaque privacy policies, it leads to:

  • Mass surveillance: Personal data is collected without meaningful consent or user understanding
  • Behavioral manipulation: Algorithms track and influence user behavior for profit
  • Loss of privacy: Intimate details of life are harvested and monetized by corporations
  • Identity theft & fraud: Data breaches expose millions to financial and personal harm
  • Discriminatory practices: Data is used to target, exclude, or discriminate against individuals

Our Fight for Personal Information Freedom

We advocate for:

  • Transparent data collection practices with explicit user consent
  • Right to know what data companies hold about you
  • Right to delete and control your personal information
  • Strict regulation against discriminatory data practices
  • Accountability and penalties for data breaches and misuse
  • Privacy by design in all technology products

What You Can Do

Join the fight for personal information freedom:

  • Use privacy-focused browsers and search engines
  • Enable two-factor authentication on your accounts
  • Regularly review and adjust privacy settings on your devices
  • Support legislation protecting digital privacy rights
  • Advocate for transparent data policies in your community
  • Share your data privacy concerns on social media
  • Donate to privacy advocacy organizations

Who We're Fighting Against

Companies with invasive data collection and surveillance practices:

  • Facebook/Meta - Collects vast amounts of personal data through tracking pixels
  • Google - Dominates web tracking and builds detailed user profiles
  • Microsoft - Integrates surveillance into Windows and OneDrive
  • AT&T - Harvests location data and sells it to third parties (NSA)
  • Verizon - Sells customer data and location information without proper consent

Who We LOVE

Companies prioritizing user privacy and data freedom:

  • DuckDuckGo - Privacy-focused search engine, no tracking
  • Firefox - Browser built with privacy and user control in mind
  • Apple - Implements privacy features and on-device processing
  • Reddit - Community-focused with less invasive data practices
  • Snapchat - Disappearing messages and privacy-first design approach
  • Linux - Open-source OS with full user control and transparency
