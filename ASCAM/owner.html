Right to Ownership

Everyone should have the legal right that consumers who purchase a product or service retains ongoing access, functionality, and control over that product for the duration implied at the time of sale.

← Back to Home

Why Right to Ownership Matters

The right to ownership is fundamental to consumer rights and people's right. When service / product providers completely take away access of people from a product they paid for through ending software support, restricting repair, and legal barriers, it leads to:

  • Corporate Control: Unfair entities and corportation gain more and more control over peoples owned products / paid for services
  • E-Waste Creation: Consumers are forced to buy new devices because old devices become instantly obselete with bricking
  • Increased Priacy: The idea that "If buying isn't ownership, Piracy isn't theft" begains to spread with action.

Our Fight for Right to Ownership

We advocate for:

  • Access to repair manuals, parts, and diagnostic tools for all consumers
  • Prohibition of anti-repair tactics like software locks and proprietary components
  • Support for independent repair shops and technicians
  • Extended device lifespans to reduce environmental impact
  • Fair pricing for genuine and third-party replacement parts

What You Can Do

Join the movement for right to repair:

  • Support and reward comapnies that practice right to ownership
  • Choose devices from manufacturers with long term support and open source drivers
  • Contact legislators and urge them to stop legalized theft
  • Share your stories and experiences on social media to spread the word
  • Donate to organizations fighting for your ownership rights
